# Code of Conduct

Sidesnap's issues and discussions should be a friendly place for everyone,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:**
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Stay on topic and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.

**Don't:**
- Insult, harass, threaten or demean anyone.
- Post sexual, hateful or violent content.
- Share other people's private information.
- Spam or advertise.

Comments that break these rules may be edited, hidden or deleted. People who
keep breaking them may be blocked from the repo.

To report a problem privately, use the **Report content** option on the
comment, or the private report link in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
