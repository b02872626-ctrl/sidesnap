# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Sidesnap.

## How it works here

- **Forks are welcome.** Make your own version: the README's
  [Make your own version](README.md#make-your-own-version) section shows what
  to change first.
- **Bug reports are welcome.** Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/b02872626-ctrl/sidesnap/issues/new/choose) with
  your macOS version, your Sidesnap version (menu › About Sidesnap) and the
  steps to make it happen.
- **Ideas are welcome** as issues too. There's no promise they'll be built,
  but they're all read.
- **Pull requests aren't accepted.** Sidesnap is kept small and is
  maintained by one person, so outside code isn't merged. If you've built
  something good, share it in your fork and mention it in an issue.

## Rules for issues

- Be kind; see the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- One problem or idea per issue. Search first to avoid duplicates.
- **Never attach screenshots that show private things** (messages,
  passwords, documents). Crop or blur them, or describe the problem instead.
- Security or privacy problems go to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public
  issues.

## Rules the code follows (keep them in your fork too)

1. **Screenshots never leave the Mac.** No uploads, no cloud, no sharing
   service.
2. **No analytics or tracking.** The update check is the only network request.
3. **Only read the screen when the person asks for a capture.**
4. **Text reading stays on the device** and can be turned off.
