# Sidesnap release notes

The newest version goes on top. `./publish.sh` reads the first `## <version>`
heading and its `- ` bullet points; they appear in the update window.

## 1.0.1
- Fixed the edge tab popping back out by itself after you clicked somewhere else.

## 1.0
- Capture an area (⌥⇧S, Space switches to a window) or the whole screen (⌥S). Every shot is copied and kept on a shelf at the right edge of your screen.
- Hover the edge to see recent shots; drag them into any app, search the text inside them, or copy text and QR codes straight from the screen (⌥⇧T).
- Edit with arrows, boxes, text, speech bubbles, blur, highlights and numbered steps. Marks stay changeable.
- Everything stays on your Mac. The only thing Sidesnap fetches is its update feed, and you can turn that off.
